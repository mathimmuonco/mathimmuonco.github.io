Research

We build mathematical models of cancer and its treatment, from the dynamics of cell populations within a tissue to the response of an individual patient. Our work follows two complementary directions. In the first, models are calibrated to clinical data to describe, and predict, how each patient responds to therapy: when the data of a patient are entered into the computer and the model is fitted to them in order to forecast the future, we obtain something like a digital twin of that patient. In the second, we study the mathematics and the biology behind the models: how ecological and evolutionary principles shape tumor growth and resistance, which dynamical behaviors a model can produce, and how its solutions can be analyzed and computed. Our main application areas are leukemias, immunotherapy and the evolution of therapy resistance.

1

CAR-T cell immunotherapy: modeling and response prediction

Models of the multiphasic kinetics of CAR-T cells, antigen-loss resistance and cytokine release syndrome, calibrated to patient data to find early predictors of relapse.

2

Cancer ecology and evolution

Ecological and evolutionary principles applied to resilience, drug resistance, adaptive therapy, phenotypic plasticity and clonal dynamics in hematopoiesis.

3

Chronic myeloid leukemia: patient-specific models, dose reduction and treatment-free remission

Mechanistic tumor-immune models fitted to individual patients, to reduce tyrosine kinase inhibitor doses and predict who sustains remission after stopping treatment.

4

Nonlinear dynamics and bifurcations

Qualitative theory of differential equations (stability, limit cycles, bifurcations of high codimension, chaos) to understand which behaviors biological models can produce.

5

PDE models and numerical analysis in mathematical oncology

Rigorous analysis (existence, uniqueness, optimal control) and numerical simulation of PDE and integro-differential models of tumor invasion, chemotherapy and CAR-T resistance.

6

Mathematical biology beyond cancer

Mechanistic models, analyzed mathematically and confronted with data, in epidemiology, plant invasion, hydrology and agriculture.
