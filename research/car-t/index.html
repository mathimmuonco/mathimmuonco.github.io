CAR-T cell immunotherapy: modeling and response prediction

CAR-T cells are engineered T lymphocytes, a "living drug" whose kinetics in the patient (expansion, contraction and persistence) differ from those of conventional drugs. Building on previous models developed with Brazilian colleagues, which describe these multiphasic kinetics, tumor resistance through antigen loss and the role of healthy B cells, we now apply and extend them to patients with B-cell lymphomas, together with the group of Malte von Bonin (TU Dresden) and Ingmar Glauche (TU Dresden). We also model the timeline of cytokine release syndrome. The models are calibrated to patient data and used to interpret what shapes the response and to look for early predictors of relapse. A recent finding is that CAR-T kinetics alone do not determine the outcome; tumor-specific features such as burden and antigen density also matter.
