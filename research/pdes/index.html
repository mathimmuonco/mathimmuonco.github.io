PDE models and numerical analysis in mathematical oncology

Spatial structure, such as tumor invasion into tissue, and population heterogeneity, such as variation in reproduction rates or in antigen expression, lead to partial differential and integro-differential equations. Together with Anderson Araujo (UFV) and collaborators, we study these models rigorously (existence and uniqueness of solutions, optimal control) and complement the analysis with numerical simulations. Examples are acid-mediated tumor invasion with chemotherapy, models with non-local diffusion, generalizations of the Lotka-Volterra competition model with continuous traits, a spatial model of plant invasion mediated by allelopathy, in which the invasion spreads from a focus as a wave, and a model of CAR-T cell resistance in which the antigen expression of tumor cells is a continuous variable, an integro-partial differential equation that we analyze and simulate numerically (with Daniela Santurio). Related interests in the theory of PDEs, nonlinear analysis and control theory are shared with Maicon Sônego (UNIFEI).
